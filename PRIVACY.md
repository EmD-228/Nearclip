# Privacy Policy

**NearClip** — last updated 27 September 2026.

NearClip is an open source app that copies text and files between your own
devices over your local network. Its source code is at
<https://github.com/EmD-228/Nearclip>.

## The short version

NearClip has no servers. Nothing you send leaves your local network, nobody
collects anything about you, and there is no account to create.

## What NearClip does with your data

**Everything stays between your devices.** Text and files travel straight from
one of your devices to another over your Wi-Fi or wired network. They are
encrypted end to end with a key that only your two paired devices hold, and they
are never uploaded anywhere.

**Nothing is sent to the developer or to any third party.** NearClip contains no
analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no tracking of any kind. The
developer cannot see what you send, when you send it, or which devices you own.

**What is stored on your device, and only on your device:**

- the name, identity key and last known address of each device you paired with;
- a history of what you sent and received, capped at the most recent items, which
  you can clear at any time from the History screen;
- your settings;
- files you received, saved to your Downloads folder, where they stay until you
  delete them.

Pairing keys are kept in the operating system's credential store (Keychain on
macOS, Credential Manager on Windows, the app's private storage on Android).
Uninstalling the app removes everything except the files already saved to your
Downloads folder.

## Permissions NearClip asks for, and why

- **Local network access** — to reach your other devices. This is the whole app.
- **Camera** (Android) — only to scan the pairing QR code another device shows.
  No image is stored or transmitted.
- **Notifications** — to tell you that something arrived.
- **Photos and files you choose to share** — read only when you pick them or
  share them to NearClip, and only to send them to your own devices.

NearClip does not use your location. It never asks for the name of your Wi-Fi
network, which is why the app shows whether a device is reachable rather than
which network it is on.

## Children

NearClip is a utility with no social features, no user content from strangers and
no advertising. It collects nothing from anyone, including children.
